# Emberwild

A fantasy open-world pixel-art RPG with a 3D perspective camera that runs in the browser. One self-contained `index.html`, no build step.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser, or turn on GitHub Pages (Settings → Pages → deploy from the `main` branch, root folder) to play it at `https://geraldbiii.github.io/Pixel-World/`.

## Controls

| Action | Keys |
| --- | --- |
| Walk forward / back | W S / ↑ ↓ |
| Turn (the world rotates with you) | A D / ← → |
| Attack | Left click |
| Talk, open chests | E / Enter |
| Drink potion | Q |
| Bag (inventory) | I |
| Character sheet | C |
| World map | M |
| Close a menu | Esc |
| Toggle sound | N |
| Orbit and tilt camera | Right-drag |
| Zoom | Scroll wheel |
| Reset camera | R |

On touch screens, on-screen buttons appear automatically. The Camera panel under the game sets tilt, orbit and zoom, and whether the camera turns with the hero.

## Features

- A finite island world ringed by the sea, with a fog-of-war world map that fills in as you explore
- Emberhold, a walled market town with gates, gate towers, cobbled streets, a fountain square, farms and over 50 buildings
- 28 townsfolk who keep daily routines: farmers work the fields, shopkeepers open and close, the bard plays at the inn by night, children play in the square, and guards stand watch, patrol the walls and fight monsters that come too close
- Shops: Brann's Forge (weapons), Hilda's Armory (armor and shields), Wren's Remedies (potions and elixirs), Tobb's Trading Post (rings, amulets, buys loot), the Gilded Ember inn (rest until morning) and the temple (daily healing)
- A 30-slot bag, 8 equipment slots (weapon, shield, head, body, hands, feet, ring, amulet) and over 50 items, with Worn, Standard, Fine, Masterwork and Legendary qualities
- Weapons with their own attack, swing speed and reach; armor that blocks a share of each hit; your hero's look changes with the armor, helmet and shield you wear
- Loot to sell: gels, bones, ectoplasm, pelts, fangs, goblin ears, trinkets, gems and relics. Loot gets better the farther you roam
- A character sheet with attributes and equipment, and records: monsters slain by type, miles walked, map explored, gold earned and spent, chests opened, hardest hit and more
- Perspective 3D view: sky and distance haze, 3D buildings, walls and mountains, sprites that scale with depth
- Day/night cycle with dynamic lighting; guards carry torches after dark
- Enemies: slimes, ember slimes, skeletons, night-only ghosts, wolf packs and goblins
- Quest line from Elder Maren, plus a skeleton bounty
