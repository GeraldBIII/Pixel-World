Tips

  • Monsters and their loot grow richer the farther you roam from Emberhold.
  • Sell loot to the shops in the market square; Tobb pays best for trinkets and gems.
  • Every building can be entered. Cupboards and chests can be searched, but taking from them is theft if someone sees you.
  • Strike a townsperson and the guards will come for you. Pay your bounty to any guard, or lie low until they stop looking.
  • Each level gives 3 attribute points. Spend them on the character sheet (C).
