Game

Move & fight

  • WS↑↓ walk forward or back
  • AD←→ turn
  • Shift sprint (uses stamina)
  • Space dodge roll: you can't be hit mid-roll
  • Left click swing your weapon
  • Q drink a healing potion

Explore

  • EEnter talk, trade, open doors, search
  • I bag (click gear to equip) · C character sheet
  • M world map · N sound
  • Esc pause menu (options, unstuck, exit) · F fullscreen
  • G god mode, for testing

Xbox controller

  • L stick walk and turn · R stick camera
  • XRT attack · B dodge roll · A use · Y potion
  • LTLB sprint · Menu pause menu · View map · RB hero
  • Press Menu on a second controller for split screen

Camera

  • Right-drag orbit and tilt (third person by default)
  • Scroll zoom in or out
  • R reset the camera

Tips

  • Monsters and their loot grow richer the farther you roam from Emberhold.
  • Sell loot to the shops in the market square; Tobb pays best for trinkets and gems.
  • Every building can be entered. Cupboards and chests can be searched, but taking from them is theft if someone sees you.
  • Strike a townsperson and the guards will come for you. Pay your bounty to any guard, or lie low until they stop looking.
  • Each level gives 3 attribute points. Spend them on the character sheet (C).
