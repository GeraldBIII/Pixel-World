Emberwild

The slimes are restless, the shrines are asleep, and the dead walk after dusk. The village elder is waiting for you.

Move & fight

  • WASDArrows walk the world
  • Left click swing your sword
  • Q drink a potion (heals 6)

Explore

  • EEnter talk to villagers, open chests
  • M show or hide the map
  • N sound on or off

Tips

  • Monsters grow tougher the farther you roam from the village.
  • Skeletons haunt forests and hills; ghosts appear only at night.
  • Tobb the merchant sells potions, sharper blades and heart vessels.
